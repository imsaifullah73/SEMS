How to use SEMS — User Manual

1

Create your account

Click Get Started and fill in your name, email and password. Verify your email with the code we send you — it takes under a minute.

2

Log in to your dashboard

After verification, log in with your email and password. You'll land on your personal dashboard showing your current financial snapshot.

3

Add your income

Go to Income, pick a category (like pocket money or part-time job), enter the amount and date, and save. That's your money coming in.

4

Record your expenses

Go to Expenses, choose a category (food, transport, study…), type the amount and description, and save. Everything you spend gets recorded.

5

Set a budget

In Budget, set a monthly limit — overall or per category. SEMS shows you how much you have left so you never overspend.

6

Watch your reports

In Reports, see your spending at a glance — where your money went and how you're doing against your budget.

💡 Tip: Use the dark/light theme toggle and manage your categories in the Categories page to keep everything organised. Your data is saved on the server, so you can log in from any device.
